Agents

One job posting. A full application.

Every document starts fresh from your master resume and is rewritten to match the role's own framing.

Master Resume BuilderNEW

Don't have a master resume yet? A guided Q&A wizard builds one from scratch — repeatable job/education entries, three original templates, smart autocomplete for cities and schools, and an optional Claude polish pass. Every other agent starts from what this one builds.

Tailored Resume

Styled DOCX with brand colors, a rewritten tagline and summary, a competency grid mapped to the JD, and bullets reframed around the role's core identity.

ATS Resume

A plain, single-column DOCX — no tables, text boxes, or color — built so applicant tracking systems can parse it linearly without losing content.

Cover Letter

Opens with their framing, not yours. Two evidence paragraphs, a differentiator specific to the role, and a short close — written in your voice, not a template's.

Interview Prep

A single-column prep doc: elevator pitch, company snapshot, your story mapped to their stated priorities, likely Q&A, smart questions to ask, and prep actions.

Application Questions

Answers freeform application questions using your tailored resume, the JD, and your profile — with a tone selector and a two-phase clarification flow.

Thank You Email

Post-interview thank-you generator with round/tone selectors and an optional interviewer name — outputs an editable email plus a DOCX synced to Drive.

Optimize

Already generated a resume or cover letter for this role? Revise it in place from a plain-English instruction — no full re-run needed.
